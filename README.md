# Industry Quest

A gamified web product that helps MBA students build industry fluency, stay current on industry developments, and practice applying their knowledge in recruiting interviews.

**🥉 3rd Place — UCLA Anderson Buildathon**

## Problem

MBA recruiting often requires students to quickly build fluency in unfamiliar industries while simultaneously keeping up with current developments and preparing for interviews.

These activities are typically fragmented across news sources, industry research, recruiting guides, and interview-preparation materials. This can make it difficult to build knowledge consistently and, more importantly, learn how to apply that knowledge during recruiting.

## Solution

Industry Quest brings industry learning and recruiting preparation into one gamified experience.

We designed the product around a **Duolingo-style learning model**, using short, repeatable activities to make building industry knowledge more engaging and habitual. Users can learn about industries, test their knowledge through daily quizzes, stay current on relevant developments, and practice applying what they learn in recruiting scenarios.

## How It Works

### 🎯 Quest

Users complete short, gamified activities designed to test and reinforce their understanding of industries, companies, terminology, and current developments.

Daily quizzes create a **Duolingo-style learning loop**, encouraging users to continuously build their industry fluency rather than relying on one-time recruiting preparation.

### 📚 Learn

Users can explore industry-specific concepts and information to strengthen their understanding before testing themselves through quests.

The learning experience is designed to make unfamiliar industries more approachable and give users a foundation they can continue building over time.

### 💼 Career Mode

Career Mode connects industry knowledge directly to recruiting preparation.

Rather than focusing only on memorizing information, users practice **how to think through interview questions**, structure their approach, and apply relevant industry knowledge in recruiting scenarios.

### 🗂️ Archive

Users can revisit previous content and learning material, making Industry Quest useful both for daily practice and deeper review when preparing for interviews.

## Key Features

- **Duolingo-style daily quizzes** for building industry knowledge and staying current on relevant developments
- Industry-specific learning experiences across multiple career paths
- Gamified progression designed to encourage consistent engagement
- Career Mode for interview preparation and applied thinking
- Personalized profiles and industry preferences
- Progress tracking and achievement systems
- Current industry news integrated into the learning experience
- Archive for revisiting previous learning content

## Product Development Approach

We focused on a simple question: **How can we make recruiting preparation something students want to come back to consistently?**

Instead of building another static repository of industry information, we designed Industry Quest around short learning loops, progression, and active practice.

For the MVP, we prioritized experiences that addressed different stages of learning:

1. **Learn** — build foundational industry knowledge
2. **Quest** — test and reinforce that knowledge through gamified activities
3. **Stay Current** — use daily content to keep up with industry developments
4. **Apply** — practice using that knowledge through Career Mode and recruiting scenarios
5. **Review** — revisit previous content through the Archive

This structure allowed us to combine industry research, current events, and interview preparation within a single experience while keeping the product approachable and engaging.
